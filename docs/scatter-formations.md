# Seeded scatter formations

Select performers while paused at a set in 2D, then choose **Scatter…** in Formation tools or **Preview scatter formation** in Commands. An empty selection never targets the ensemble implicitly. Scatter destinations remain an editable live formation on that set.

Choose a rectangle, ellipse or polygon. The first two use opposite bounding corners; polygons use 3–32 ordered vertices, including concave outlines. Drag the square region handles or select a Region vertex and edit its numeric X/Y coordinates. Split edge inserts its midpoint; Delete vertex reconnects neighbors with at least three vertices remaining. Crossing/touching non-adjacent polygon edges, degenerate regions and out-of-field vertices are rejected with the last valid values restored.

**Scatter seed** controls repeatability. Choose minimum center-to-center spacing, uniform/clustered density and, for clustered arrangements, strength from 0 to 1. Changing the region, settings, membership or positional/roster assignment leaves the current dots visible and requires **Regenerate**. **Reroll** selects a new stored seed and regenerates. Neither action accepts a partial arrangement or reduces the requested spacing. A failed bounded search asks for more area, smaller spacing or fewer members; it does not prove that no possible packing exists.

Select a Scatter member and pin it to retain its shown position on regeneration. Pins participate in spacing, including checks against each other. Pins follow performer identities through explicit reassignment and membership changes. A pin outside a newly edited region must be moved, unpinned or enclosed before generation can succeed. A pinned dot can be moved explicitly in the preview; its new location becomes the pin on the next generation.

Circular field handles and Scatter dot X/Y fields override individual dots while retaining membership and checking region/spacing constraints. Ordinary per-dot editing after closing the preview follows the existing live-formation policy: the edited dot detaches and other members keep their slots. Vacant slots remain reserved, including in generation and spacing, until **Redistribute members** explicitly closes gaps and requires regeneration. Removing a pinned member also removes its pin. Unrelated performers and their identities/sections retain their values.

Incoming blue dashed paths and outgoing gold dotted paths use the same movement evaluator as playback. The shared worker checks both adjacent moves for spacing/crossing and excessive stride. **Show entry/exit paths and flags** hides or shows paths/highlight rings for crowded casts; checks and flag counts remain active. Minimum destination spacing applies to scatter slots; it is not a clearance guarantee for transitions or against unrelated performers. Detailed travel definitions retain their values; invalid endpoint edits fail through the existing project validation.

**Apply formation** accepts the entire preview as one named undo transaction. **Cancel/Escape** restores the production; Escape during a drag first restores that gesture's starting preview and pending-generation state. Saved live formations support Edit, copy-to-set previews, ownership conflicts, detachment, undo/redo and native save/reopen. Format v10 stores accepted dot positions, pins, region/settings and seed; reopening never regenerates or relies on a future random algorithm matching the current one. Older applications reject this wire version instead of discarding the metadata. Earlier productions keep their existing format.

Generation version 1 uses an FNV-1a hash of UTF-16 seed units and Mulberry32, independent of wall time and Math.random. Candidate rejection uses a spatial hash and a global budget of 200,000 attempts, including clustered-center selection. Clustered mode uses two seeded Gaussian centers, sigma = smaller bounding dimension / 10 (minimum .1 yd), and a .005 background acceptance floor blended with the requested strength. Density never overrides spacing. After pins are installed, unpinned destinations sort by X/Y and fill the frozen assignment slots. Regions require at least .1 yd width/depth and .01 square yard of area; polygon edges require .01 yd. Geometric spacing/boundary comparisons use a documented 1e-8 yard tolerance. Current limits are 1–500 slots.
